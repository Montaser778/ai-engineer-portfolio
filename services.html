Process

Four steps, every time

01

Free scoping call

A short call to understand the problem, the constraint, and whether this is a good fit before anything is proposed.

02

Written proposal

Scope, timeline and price in writing, broken into milestones that can each be demoed on their own.

03

Visible increments

Working software at each milestone, not a single delivery at the end of the engagement.

04

Handover with support window

Documentation and a fixed support window after delivery, so the handover is not the end of accountability.
