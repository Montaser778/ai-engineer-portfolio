Montaser Hussam

AI/ML engineer based in Gaza, Palestine, working remotely with teams across time zones.

Working method

Scope narrow first. A voice or agent feature is broken into the smallest slice that can be demoed end to end, then widened once that slice is measured and working — not designed fully up front and built in one long push.

Every architectural choice states its cost. A faster integration that cannot survive a duplicate webhook, or a lower-latency endpointing threshold that misfires in a noisy room, gets named as a trade-off rather than presented as free.

What he is looking for

Teams building a real-time voice product, an agent-based workflow, or a production LLM feature that needs to survive contact with real users and real network conditions — not a proof of concept that stays a proof of concept.
