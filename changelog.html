What changed, and why

This site is maintained, not shipped once and left alone.

15 Aug 2026v1.1.0
  • Added bilingual EN/AR toggle with full RTL support
  • Added dark/light/system theme, applied to both 3D scenes
  • Added client-side search wired into the command palette
  • Split notes into individual pages, added an RSS feed
  • Added the multi-agent case study as a second full write-up
  • Added a working contact form with honeypot and time-trap spam protection
  • Added booking CTA and a timezone converter
  • Added offline support, security headers and a CI workflow

Why: a single case study reads as a lucky project; a working form and bilingual support are what turn a browse into a sent brief.

2 Jul 2026v1.0.1
  • Fixed particle point-size formula in the background scene (was rendering as one oversized blob)
  • Added a low-alpha constellation-line layer tracing each shape
  • Restored horizontal padding on the hero and page-head sections
  • Tuned canvas opacity and vignette for text contrast

Why: the 3D layer should read as a deliberate motif, not compete with body text for attention.

18 Jun 2026v1.0.0
  • Initial launch: ten pages, background particle field, hero workstation model, six live demos

Why: the baseline site — every page built to move a visitor toward a project brief.
